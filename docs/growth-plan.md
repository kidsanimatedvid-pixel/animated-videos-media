# Growth plan — سُهَيْل وأصحابه (22 Nov 2026)

## What the research says
- **Search demand (vidIQ, Saudi Arabia, Nov 2026):** "قصص اطفال" ≈ 78k searches/month; **"قصص اطفال قبل النوم" ≈ 23k/month and rising +96%**; "حكايات عربية" ≈ 35k; "قصص أطفال" ≈ 20k; "كرتون عربي" ≈ 7.7k. Long-tail phrases ("قصص اطفال قصيرة", "حكايات اطفال") have little volume but low competition.
- **What breaks out (vidIQ outliers, Arabic bedtime stories):** small channels get 50–550× their usual views with (1) a funny mystery in the title ("من أكل كيك ريم في منتصف الليل؟ 🎂😂 لغز مضحك" — 557×), (2) a rescue/adventure with an animal ("…تنقذان السلحفاة الصغيرة على البحر 🌊" — 293×), (3) a classic fable retold ("الحمار والأسد الجائع" — 51×), (4) a question that creates suspense ("ساعة سندريلا توقفت! هل تصل إلى الحفلة؟"). Almost all add "| قصص اطفال قبل النوم".
- **Timing:** general benchmarks put Saudi YouTube peaks in the evening (around 6 PM, and late evening), and kids content around school and bedtime routines (morning, mid-afternoon, early evening). Saudi weekends are Friday–Saturday. Shorts are pushed by the feed for days, so consistency matters more than the exact minute.

## Decisions
1. **Posting times (KSA):** LEARN 15:30 Sun–Thu (after school) and 10:00 Fri–Sat; STORY 19:00 every day (bedtime). Our moon narrator Qamra makes "bedtime story" a natural brand fit.
2. **Titles:** question/surprise + emoji + "| قصص اطفال قبل النوم #shorts" for stories; "<topic> مع سُهَيْل + emoji | تعليم الحروف للاطفال #shorts" for lessons.
3. **Story mix:** rotate funny mysteries, animal rescues, retold fables (with our cast and Gulf settings) and everyday values.
4. **Two series = two playlists** (حكايات سُهَيْل, تعلَّم مع سُهَيْل) so viewers binge.
5. **Approval stays human:** every video is approved before it posts.

## Next growth moves (in order)
1. Weeks 1–2: post daily, no changes; collect data.
2. Week 2 review: compare STORY vs LEARN, mysteries vs values, and first-3-second retention.
3. Week 3+: test one variable at a time (hook style, length 35 s vs 50 s, title pattern).
4. When 10+ stories exist: make a long-form compilation ("قصص اطفال قبل النوم مجمعة") from the Shorts — long bedtime compilations are what the biggest Arabic kids channels rank with, and they bring search traffic back to the Shorts.
5. Later: add TikTok/Instagram aimed at parents if YouTube growth is steady.
