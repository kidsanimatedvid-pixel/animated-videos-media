## 2. Settings → Channel
- **Keywords:** `business documentary, how companies make money, business explained, corporate strategy, company history, rise and fall, business model, business case study, economics explained, business shorts`
- **Country:** your actual country of residence. Don't change it to target viewers.
- **Advanced settings → Audience:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids". This is the channel default for **new** uploads. Leave each old kids video as it is (Made for Kids).

## 3. Settings → Upload defaults
- Visibility: Private, so nothing goes public by accident (vidIQ sets public plus the schedule per video).
- Category: Education.
- Video language and title/description language: English.
- Description template: `Sources:` · `Educational content — not financial advice.`
- Altered content: "No". Our animation is stylised; tick "Yes" for any video that ever includes a realistic synthetic person or event.

## 4. The 7 kids Shorts: recommendation is to make them Private (not delete)
**What happens if they go Private:**
- They disappear from the channel page, the feeds and search. Their ~21 views stop growing.
- Nothing is lost. Views, the Made-for-Kids flag and the files stay, and you can switch them back to Public at any time.
- The 4 subscribers stay.
- New English viewers won't land on Arabic preschool videos, and YouTube's recommendation system stops mixing the two audiences.

**What happens if you leave them public:** the channel looks inconsistent to new viewers ("Arabic kids cartoons?") and the audience signals stay mixed. With only 7 videos and ~21 views, there is no upside to keeping them public.

I can do this for you through vidIQ (privacy only; nothing is deleted) if you reply **"make the kids videos private"**.
